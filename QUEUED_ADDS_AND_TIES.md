# Queued additions and catalog routing

LIVE: activated 18 October 2026 after archive 354244f72ceed3859e02f324095abd28 was verified. Separate service PID 58979. Historical staging notes below are superseded by the final activation record.

Two causes: (1) the router omitted ties/neckties, sending “show me ties” into generic GPT context lookup where the cart tool was available; (2) a single pending-add slot replaced earlier selected items. The prior recovery patch restored reselectability but did not queue multiple intentions. This change replaces that slot with an ordered, conversation/customer-owned queue.

Each queue entry preserves product, exact size, originating constraints, displayed price, event/card identity and stable storefront form idempotency key. Every entry means one unit. Repeated clicks on the same card do not increase quantity. Each is revalidated through the controlled browser after authorization; a failed item receives its own message and does not discard later entries. Unknown mutation outcomes are not automatically retried. Price/stock failures can be retried by selecting the card after reviewing current availability; changed prices require a new search. Authorized additions still happen exclusively through actual storefront forms, never purchase APIs or injected cookies. No automatic checkout.

Chat supports “show pending adds”, “cancel queued item 2”, and “cancel add” (cancel all). Permission continuation preserves the queue; a completed grant drains it once in selection order. Ordinary searches and refinements do not invoke bag reads or checkout. Same card quantity increases are deliberately not inferred; use FORME's bag quantity controls for deliberate changes. Clearing/archiving the conversation explicitly cancels temporary queued intentions.

Ties/neckties are now recognized as an accessories catalog search, including follow-up budget/color refinements. Explicit discovery is excluded from the generic context-tool route; genuine bag language and typos remain supported. The public live catalog has no stocked ties, so the result is honest zero-match text, not an empty-cart response. The existing red-tie availability demo is separate. Empty ties/accessories facets remain selectable. No live catalog stock was invented. The queue test supplies a stocked tie only in its disposable fixture.
